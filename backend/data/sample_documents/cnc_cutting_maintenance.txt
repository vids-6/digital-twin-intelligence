NORTHSTAR MAINTENANCE MANUAL — CNC CUTTING UNIT
Document code: MM-CNC-021 | Revision 2

SPINDLE TEMPERATURE AND LUBRICATION
Unusual spindle temperature can result from insufficient lubrication, excessive cutting load, tool wear, cooling-system restriction, or a failing bearing. Confirm the sensor reading and operating conditions before diagnosing. Review lubrication intervals and coolant flow against the machine manufacturer's instructions.

VIBRATION AND CUT QUALITY
Increasing vibration may correlate with tool imbalance, worn spindle bearings, loose tooling, or workpiece clamping issues. Stop the spindle before checking tooling or fixtures. Follow the machine-specific isolation procedure. Do not bypass interlocks.

MAINTENANCE ACTIONS
Inspect coolant level and flow, check tool condition and clamping, review recent alarms, and compare spindle temperature trends with baseline data. A qualified technician should inspect bearings if symptoms persist. Document the findings and test the unit before release.
